The organizing team and volunteers act in good faith to make the volunteer
dinner event safe and welcoming, with care for the well-being of everyone who
attends or helps out.

I understand that volunteering at the volunteer dinner event may involve risks,
including cuts, burns, slips, falls, and other injuries, particularly when
working in or around the kitchen.

I voluntarily accept these inherent risks and agree to follow all safety
instructions, practice proper hygiene, use equipment responsibly, and promptly
report any unsafe conditions or injuries.

## Kitchen and service rules

You must strictly follow these rules when in the kitchen and when serving food.
They are both for your safety and to ensure proper food safety.

### A hair net is required

You must wear a hair net at all times when in the kitchen or when serving food.
If you have an unruly beard or too much facial hair you will need to wear a
beard net. Anyone with long hair or a pony tail must have it tied up.
We'll have hair nets available for you at the venue.

### Proper attire must be worn

Proper kitchen attire must be worn when in the kitchen. Closed-toe shoes without
heels, long pants and long sleeves are required to protect you from burns.

### Clean hands and gloves

Hands must be thoroughly washed for working in the kitchen. They should also be
periodically rewashed. If you are handling food, you must wear food-safe nitrile
gloves. Do not touch or scratch your face, hair, or other parts of your body
without rewashing your hands and changing your gloves. If you cough or sneeze,
do so into your elbow and then rewash your hands and change your gloves.

### Take breaks

If you are tired or need a break, please ask, and then take one. Please do not
disappear without informing someone first that you’re leaving your station so
that it can be covered and so that food does not burn, get forgotten in an oven
or left out.

### At your own risk

If you are not comfortable working in the kitchen environment, please say so
and we will try and find other duties for you in the dining hall. You are
working here at your own risk. Be aware of sources of heat and steam and heavy
items and slippery floors. If you are not sure how something works or how to
operate something safely, please ask.
